高中英语从句与特殊句式

从句与特殊句式

“xx从句”就是指一个句子承担xx的功能，比如说，定语从句，就是一个句子承担定语的功能

一、定语从句 ★

1. 核心概念

定语从句 = 形容词性从句，修饰名词或代词（先行词），相当于一个”长的形容词”。

The book that I borrowed is interesting. （我借的那本书很有趣。）

2. 关系词系统（选择关系词的三大步骤）

第一步：确定先行词

  • 人：who, whom, that, whose
  • 物/动物：which, that, whose
  • 时间：when, that
  • 地点：where, that
  • 原因：why, that

第二步：判断从句成分

关系词 功能 例句
who 作主语（代人） The manwho is speaking is our teacher.
whom 作宾语（代人，可省略） The girl (whom) you met yesterday is my sister.
whose 作定语（表所属，”…的”） The boywhose father is a doctor won the prize.
which 作主语或宾语（代物） The bookwhich lies on the desk is mine.（主语）
that 作主语或宾语（代人或物） Everythingthat he said was true.
when 作时间状语 I’ll never forget the daywhen we first met.
where 作地点状语 This is the housewhere I was born.
why 作原因状语 That’s the reasonwhy I was late.

第三步：特殊规则与限制

只能用 that 的情况：
  1. 先行词为不定代词（all, everything, nothing, little, much等）

    All that glitters is not gold.

  2. 先行词被序数词或最高级修饰

    This is the best movie that I have ever seen.

  3. 先行词被the very, the only, the same等修饰

    He is the only person that can help us.

  4. 先行词既有人又有物

    They talked about the people and things that they remembered.

只能用 which 的情况：
  1. 非限制性定语从句中（逗号隔开）

    The book, which I bought yesterday, is very useful.

  2. 介词后（介词+which）

    This is the room in which he was born.

as 的特殊用法：
  1. 引导非限制性定语从句，常代整个主句，位置灵活

    As we all know, the earth is round.
    The earth is round, as we all know.

  2. 与such, the same连用

    Such people as you describe are rare.

3. 限制性 vs 非限制性定语从句

对比维度 限制性定语从句 非限制性定语从句
意义 对先行词限制、特指，去掉后句意不完整 补充说明先行词，去掉后句意仍完整
形式 无逗号与主句隔开 有逗号与主句隔开
关系词 that可用 that不可用
翻译 “…的” “这/该…”（常译为一个独立句子）
例句 My brotherwho lives in Beijing will come.（我有多个兄弟，特指在北京的） My brother,who lives in Beijing, will come.（我只有一个兄弟，补充说明他在北京）

4. 高考高频考点

  1. 介词+关系代词结构

    • 判断介词：看从句动词短语搭配或根据意思判断

      This is the house in which he lived.（live in the house）

  2. 关系词省略条件：

    • 关系代词作宾语时可省略
    • 关系副词when, why在the time when, the reason why结构中可省略

      I still remember the day (when) we first met.

  3. whose的转换：whose + n. = the + n. + of which/whom

    The building whose roof is red = The building the roof of which is red.

二、名词性从句

1. 四大类型及识别方法

① 主语从句

  • 位置：句首（多数情况）
  • 识别：从句作整个句子的主语
  • 例句：

    That he passed the exam surprised everyone.（他通过了考试让所有人惊讶）
    Whether we will go depends on the weather.（我们是否去取决于天气）

② 宾语从句

  • 位置：及物动词/介词后
  • 识别：从句作动词或介词的宾语
  • 例句：

    I know that he is honest.
    He is interested in what I said.

③ 表语从句

  • 位置：系动词（be, seem, appear等）后
  • 识别：从句作表语，说明主语是什么
  • 例句：

    The fact is that we have lost the game.
    The question is whether we can finish on time.

④ 同位语从句

  • 位置：抽象名词（fact, news, idea, belief等）后

  • 识别：解释说明前面名词的具体内容（=前面名词的内容）

  • 连接词：常用that（不充当成分，不可省略），whether

  • 例句：

    The news that our team won made us happy.（消息的内容是我们队赢了）

    vs 定语从句：

    The news that he told me was exciting.（他告诉我的消息，that作told的宾语）

2. 引导词系统（三类）

第一类：连词（不充当成分，只起连接作用）

  • that：无词义（宾语从句中可省略）

    He said (that) he would come.

  • whether/if：是否（whether可引导所有名词从句，if只能引导宾语从句且不与or not连用）

    • 只能用whether的情况：
      1. 引导主语从句放句首

        Whether he will come is unknown.（✓）
        If he will come is unknown.（✗）

      2. 与or not连用

        I wonder whether or not he agrees.

      3. 介词后

        It depends on whether we have time.

      4. 引导表语/同位语从句

        The question is whether we can afford it.

第二类：连接代词（在从句中作主/宾/表/定语）

  • what：什么，…的东西（= the thing(s) which）

    I don’t know what you mean.（what作mean的宾语）

  • who/whom：谁

    Who will go is not decided.（who作主语）

  • which：哪一个

    Tell me which book you prefer.（which作定语）

  • whose：谁的

    I wonder whose bag this is.

第三类：连接副词（在从句中作状语）

  • when：什么时候
  • where：在哪里
  • why：为什么
  • how：如何，怎样

Please tell me when we shall meet.（when作时间状语）
That’s why I was late.（why作原因状语）

3. 语序与时态

  1. 从句一律用陈述语序（主语在前，谓语在后）

    ❌ I don’t know where is he.
    ✅ I don’t know where he is.

  2. 时态呼应：主句过去时，从句一般用相应过去时（客观真理除外）

    He said he was tired.（主句said过去时）
    He said the earth is round.（客观真理不变）

4. 高考高频考点

  1. it作形式主语/宾语

    • 主语从句过长时，用it作形式主语

      It is obvious that he is lying.

    • 常用句型：
      • It is + adj. + that… (important, necessary, strange等)
      • It is + n. + that… (a pity, a fact, good news等)
      • It + be动词 + 过去分词 + that… (said, reported, believed等)
      • It + 不及物动词 + that… (seems, happens, appears等)

  2. what的万能用法

    • 可表示”所…的事物”（= the thing(s) which）

      What he needs is more practice.（他所需要的是更多练习）

    • what vs that 区别：

      I know that he is honest.（that无词义，不作成分）
      I know what he wants.（what=”他想要的东西”，作wants的宾语）

  3. 同位语从句 vs 定语从句的区分（高考常考）

    • 功能不同：同位语解释名词内容；定语修饰限制名词
    • that作用：同位语中that是连词，不作成分；定语中that是关系代词，作成分
    • 替换测试：同位语可用”The fact is that…”测试

      The news that our team won excited us.（同位语：消息内容是”我们队赢了”）
      The news that you told me excited us.（定语：你告诉我的消息，that作told的宾语）

三、状语从句 ★

1. 九大类型及常用连词

① 时间状语从句

  • 常用连词：when, while, as, before, after, since, until, as soon as, the moment等
  • 核心区别：
    • when：从句动作可先于、后于或与主句同时发生

      When I arrived, he was sleeping.（我到时他正在睡）

    • while：强调主从句动作同时持续（常进行时）

      While I was reading, he was watching TV.

    • as：强调”一边…一边”，或”随着”

      As she grew older, she became wiser.

② 条件状语从句

  • 常用连词：if, unless（= if not）, as/so long as, provided that等
  • 时态规则：主将从现（主句将来时，从句现在时表将来）

    If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the trip.

  • if vs unless：

    We will go if it doesn’t rain.
    = We will go unless it rains.（除非下雨，否则我们会去）

③ 原因状语从句

  • 常用连词：because, since, as, now that（既然）
  • 语气强度：because > since/as > for（for是并列连词）
  • because vs because of：

    He was late because it rained heavily.（从句）
    He was late because of the heavy rain.（短语）

④ 让步状语从句

  • 常用连词：though/although, even if/though, as, while（尽管）, no matter + 疑问词
  • though/although 不与but连用（但可与yet, still连用）

    Although it was raining, yet we went out.

  • as引导的倒装让步：Adj./Adv./N./V. + as + 主语 + 谓语

    Child as he is, he knows a lot.（注意：单数名词前不用冠词）

⑤ 目的状语从句

  • 常用连词：so that, in order that, lest（以免）
  • 常含情态动词：can, could, may, might, should

    Speak clearly so that they may understand you.

⑥ 结果状语从句

  • 常用连词：so…that, such…that
  • 结构区别：
    • so + adj./adv. + that

      He ran so fast that I couldn’t catch up.

    • such + (a/an) + adj. + n. + that

      It was such a hot day that we all went swimming.

    • so many/few + 可数名词复数 + that
    • so much/little + 不可数名词 + that

⑦ 比较状语从句

  • 常用连词：than, as…as, not so/as…as
  • 结构：

    He is taller than I (am).（口语中常用me）
    She is as beautiful as her sister (is).

⑧ 方式状语从句

  • 常用连词：as, as if/though（好像）
  • as if/though的时态：
    • 可能真实：用正常时态

      It looks as if it is going to rain.

    • 非真实（虚拟）：用过去式（现在）或过去完成（过去）

      He talks as if he knew everything.（实际上不知道）

⑨ 地点状语从句

  • 常用连词：where, wherever
  • 用法：

    Where there is a will, there is a way.
    I will follow you wherever you go.

2. 重点连词辨析

(1) when, while, as

  • when：时间点或时间段，可表突然发生

    I was about to leave when the phone rang.（突然）

  • while：强调对比或同时持续

    While I like the story, I don’t like the ending.（对比）

  • as：强调变化或”随着”

    As time went by, things got better.

(2) because, since, as, for

  • because：直接原因，回答why，可单独使用

    —Why were you absent?
    —Because I was ill.

  • since/as：已知原因，常放句首，语气较弱

    Since everyone is here, let’s begin.

  • for：补充说明原因，是并列连词，不放句首

    It must have rained, for the ground is wet.

(3) though/although, even though, as

  • though/although：最常用，可与yet, still连用
  • even though：语气更强
  • as：必须倒装

(4) if, unless

  • unless = if not（除非…否则）

    You will fail unless you work harder.
    = You will fail if you don’t work harder.

3. 状语从句省略

条件：从句主语与主句主语一致，且谓语含be动词

  1. 省略主语和be

    When (he was) asked about it, he kept silent.

  2. 固定结构：if/unless/when necessary/possible

    I’ll help you if possible.

四、特殊句式

1. 强调句（It is/was…that/who…）

① 基本结构

  • It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 剩余部分
  • 判断方法：去掉It is/was…that/who，剩余部分能组成完整句子

    It was yesterday that I met him.
    → I met him yesterday.（完整句子）

② 可强调的成分

  • 主语：It is I who/that am to blame.
  • 宾语：It is English that he teaches.
  • 状语（时间/地点/方式等）：

    It was in Shanghai that he was born.
    It is by bus that she goes to work.

③ 特殊形式

  • 强调not…until：It is/was not until…that…

    He didn’t come back until midnight.
    → It was not until midnight that he came back.

  • 疑问句式：

    Was it in 2010 that you graduated?
    Who was it that called just now?

④ 强调句 vs 主语从句

  • 强调句：It is…that…为框架，that后为完整句

    It is the book that I want.（强调the book）

  • 主语从句：It is…that…中that引导从句作主语

    It is obvious that he is lying.（that he is lying是主语）

2. 倒装句

① 完全倒装（主谓全部颠倒）

条件：地点/方向副词或介词短语放句首，且主语是名词（不是代词）

  • Here/There/Now/Then + 动词 + 主语（名词）

    Here comes the bus.（名词主语）
    **Here he comes.**（代词主语，不倒装）

  • 地点状语提前：

    In front of the house stands a tall tree.

  • 表语提前（尤其分词/形容词）：

    Gone are the days when we played together.

② 部分倒装（助动词/情态动词/be动词提到主语前）

条件：

  1. 否定词或半否定词放句首：

    • never, seldom, hardly, scarcely, rarely, little, nowhere
    • not, not only, no sooner…than, hardly…when

    Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.
    Not only does he speak English, but he also speaks French.

    注意：not only…but also连接两个主语时，不倒装

    Not only he but also I am interested in it.

  2. Only + 状语放句首：

    Only in this way can we solve the problem.
    Only when he came back did I know the truth.

    注意：only修饰主语时，不倒装

    Only he knows the answer.

  3. So/Such…that结构中，so/such提前：

    So fast did he run that I couldn’t catch up.

  4. 虚拟条件句省略if：were/had/should提前

    Were I you, I would accept.（= If I were you）
    Had you come earlier, you would have met him.

  5. as/though引导的让步状语从句：

    Child as he is, he knows a lot.

3. 虚拟语气（重点在if条件句）

① 三种基本形式

时间 if从句谓语形式 主句谓语形式 例句
与现在事实相反 过去式（be用were） would/could/might/should + 动词原形 If Iwere you, I would accept it.
与过去事实相反 had + 过去分词 would/could/might/should + have + 过去分词 If youhad come earlier, you would have met her.
与将来事实可能相反 1. 过去式 <br>2. should + 动词原形 <br>3. were to + 动词原形 would/could/might/should + 动词原形 If itrained tomorrow, we would cancel the trip.

② 混合虚拟

主句和从句时间不一致

If you had taken my advice（过去）, you wouldn’t be in trouble now（现在）.

③ 省略if的倒装虚拟

将were, had, should提前，省略if

Were I you (= If I were you), I would go.
Had you come earlier (= If you had come earlier), you would have met him.
Should it rain tomorrow (= If it should rain tomorrow), we would cancel it.

④ 其他虚拟语气句型

  1. wish后的宾语从句

    • 现在：过去式（be用were）

      I wish I were taller.

    • 过去：had + 过去分词

      I wish I had studied harder.

    • 将来：would/could + 动词原形

      I wish it would stop raining.

  2. as if/though从句（与wish类似）

  3. 表示建议、要求、命令等词的从句（should + 动词原形，should可省）

    • 动词：suggest, insist, demand, require, order, propose等

      He suggested that we (should) leave early.

    • 名词：suggestion, proposal, demand, requirement等

      His suggestion is that we (should) leave early.

    • 形容词：important, necessary, essential, strange等

      It is important that he (should) be informed.

  4. **It is (high) time that…**（早该做某事了）

    It is time that we went to bed.（用过去式）

综合应用与易错点总结

1. 从句嵌套识别

复杂句中常包含多层从句，需逐层分析：

The book that you lent me [定语从句] is about what happened in 2008 [宾语从句] when I was born [定语从句].

2. 长难句分析三步法

  1. 找主句主干（主谓宾/主系表）
  2. 识别从句类型（看引导词和位置）
  3. 确定修饰关系（哪个从句修饰哪个词）

3. 常见陷阱

  1. 定语从句中关系词选择：分析先行词和从句成分
  2. 名词性从句语序：永远用陈述语序
  3. 状语从句时态：条件/时间状语从句中用现在时代替将来时
  4. 强调句判断：用”去掉法”检验
  5. 虚拟语气形式：严格按时间选择对应结构
  6. 倒装条件：注意only修饰状语时才倒装
